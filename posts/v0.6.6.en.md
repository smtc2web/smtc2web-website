## Added

> ### Linux ARM platform support
>
> Adds support for the Linux ARM platform
>
> - Supports Kunpeng, Phytium, Rockchip, and other domestic ARM chips
>
> - Supports Broadcom, Qualcomm, MediaTek, and future NVIDIA overseas ARM chips

> ### Visual animations
>
> Adds polished visual animations

> ### Hot reload on theme switch
>
> After the update, switching themes triggers an automatic browser refresh

## Removed

> ### The dev command
>
> This command has been replaced by [smtc2web-dev](https://github.com/smtc2web/smtc2web-dev.git)

## Fixed

> ### Toaster appearing at the bottom
>
> After the fix, the Toaster is back in the top-right corner of the window.
